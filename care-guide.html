Care Guide

Comfort that lasts, cycle after cycle

Simple guidance for choosing, wearing, washing, and storing your HailaFlo period underwear.

Absorbency & fit Washing Care routine
01 Absorbency & fit

Choose protection for your flow, not just the day

Your flow can change throughout your cycle. Match each pair to when and how you plan to wear it, and keep a higher-absorbency option available for heavier hours or overnight use.

LightSpotting, discharge, lighter cycle days, or backup protection.
ModerateEveryday flow, work, school, and regular daytime wear.
HeavyHeavier days, longer outings, and overnight protection.

Check the fit

  • The waistband should sit securely without digging in.
  • The gusset should lie close to the body without gaps.
  • Leg openings should feel snug but should not leave painful marks.
  • If you are between sizes, use your hip measurement and preferred fit as your guide.
02 Washing

A gentle wash protects the absorbent layers

  1. 1
    Rinse cold

    After wear, rinse under cold water until the water begins to run clear. Avoid hot water, which can set stains.

  2. 2
    Wash gently

    Machine wash on a cool, gentle cycle with similar colors and mild detergent. A mesh laundry bag can reduce fabric stress.

  3. 3
    Air dry

    Reshape the garment and hang or lay it flat in a ventilated place. Make sure it is completely dry before storing.

03 Everyday routine

Build a routine that keeps each pair ready

A small rotation makes care easier. Most people begin with three to five pairs, with a mix of absorbencies for lighter days, heavier flow, and overnight wear.

Change when needed

If the surface starts to feel damp or the gusset feels full, change into a fresh pair.

Dry completely

Moisture trapped during storage can cause odor. Store only when every layer is fully dry.

Store with care

Keep pairs unfolded or loosely folded in a clean, dry drawer away from direct heat.

When performance changes: If a pair begins leaking sooner than expected, check the fit, review whether your flow has changed, and make sure fabric softener has not coated the absorbent fibers.
