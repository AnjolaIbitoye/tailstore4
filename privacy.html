Privacy at Hailaflo

At Hailaflo, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, purchase our 4-layer period underwear, or interact with our brand across multi-channel platforms like TikTok Shop.

This policy is written in strict compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), as our distribution operations extend to Germany.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when purchasing our products or communicating with us. This includes:

  • Identity and Contact Data: Your full name, delivery address, billing address, email address, and phone number.
  • Transaction and Financial Data: Details about payments made by you and products you have purchased from us. Note: We do not directly store your credit card or bank account details; all payments are securely processed by authorized third-party payment gateways (e.g., Paystack, Flutterwave, or Shopify Payments).
  • Technical and Usage Data: Your IP address, browser type, device information, and details about how you browse and interact with our online storefront.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data under lawful bases recognized by the NDPA and GDPR to fulfill our contract with you and improve our services. We use this data to:

  • Process, package, and ship your orders in plain, discreet packaging.
  • Manage your customer account, process secure payments, and handle returns or size exchanges.
  • Send you order updates, tracking information, and customer support communications.
  • With your explicit consent, send you promotional emails, product launch newsletters, or targeted advertisements on social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok.

3. Data Sharing and Cross-Border Transfers (Germany Distribution)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. To fulfill your orders and manage our international logistics, your data is securely shared with:

  • International Distribution & Warehouse Partners: Because our distribution center is located in Germany, your identity and shipping data (name, delivery address, phone number) are securely transferred to our German distributor to pack and dispatch your orders. This cross-border data transfer is protected by strict data processing agreements that meet European GDPR security standards.
  • E-Commerce and Marketplace Platforms: If you purchase Hailaflo garments via TikTok Shop, your order details are securely synchronized with our backend systems to facilitate fast processing and shipping.
  • Logistics and Delivery Partners: Trusted local and international courier services to ensure your orders arrive safely at your doorstep.
  • Marketing and Analytics Partners: Analytical services like Google Analytics and social media advertising pixels (such as the TikTok Pixel) to optimize your browsing experience.

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement strict physical, electronic, and administrative security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation. In accordance with Nigerian and EU data laws, we only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the business purposes for which it was collected, or to satisfy legal, accounting, and regulatory reporting requirements.

5. Your Legal Rights (NDPA & GDPR)

Depending on your location, you hold specific data rights regarding your personal information under the NDPA 2023 or EU GDPR. You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify or update inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") by requesting that we permanently delete your data, provided it is no longer required for legal or contractual fulfillment.
  • Data Portability to request a transfer of your data to another service provider.
  • Withdraw Consent at any time from our marketing emails by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any message.
