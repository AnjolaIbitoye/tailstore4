Hailaflo Period Underwear: Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about absorbency, fit, materials, care, and everyday use.

Product Basics & Protection

What is Hailaflo Period Underwear and how does it work?

Hailaflo Period Underwear is the ultimate reusable period underwear designed to replace traditional pads and tampons. Engineered with a built-in, 4-layer gusset, our eco-friendly menstrual products absorb your flow directly, giving you complete peace of mind and sustainable comfort all day long.

Our advanced fabric technology features four high-performance layers that work together seamlessly to keep you dry, fresh, and confident:

  • Moisture-Wicking Top Layer: Rapidly pulls fluid away from your skin, ensuring you feel completely dry, fresh, and comfortable.
  • Absorbent Core: Traps and locks in liquid using advanced capillary action to handle everything from light spotting to a heavy flow.
  • Waterproof Leak-Proof Barrier: Prevents blood and fluid from seeping through, protecting your clothes and bedsheets from stains.
  • Premium Outer Fabric Layer: Provides the final structural layer of security while looking and feeling exactly like high-quality, everyday underwear.

Switch to Hailaflo for reliable, sustainable period care that protects both you and the planet.

Can period underwear leak?

Hailaflo period underwear is highly effective and designed to give you ultimate leak-proof protection. However, like any menstrual product, it can leak if it reaches full capacity or if it fits poorly.

To prevent leaks, it is essential to choose the right fit and the correct absorbency level for your menstrual cycle. We offer a range of styles designed for everything from light spotting to a heavy flow.

How do I find the perfect fit?

To guarantee leak-proof protection, your period underwear should fit snugly around your legs and waist without digging in. If the leg openings are too loose, gaps can cause leaks even if the underwear isn't full. We recommend checking our detailed size guide and measuring your hips to find your true match.

Care & Performance

Would my period pant smell?

No, Hailaflo period underwear will not smell while you are wearing it. Unlike conventional disposable pads that feature plastic backings that trap heat, sweat, and moisture, Hailaflo is crafted from OEKO-TEX® certified, highly breathable fabrics.

Our advanced 4-layer technology actively prevents odour from developing:

  • The Moisture-Wicking Top Layer rapidly pulls menstrual fluid away from your skin and dries almost instantly.
  • By eliminating the damp environment where bacteria usually thrive, the underwear naturally stays clean, fresh, and completely odour-free for up to 8 to 12 hours.

Pro-Tip to Keep Them Fresh

To ensure your underwear never develops a post-wash, musty scent, never use fabric softeners. Softeners leave a microscopic film on the fabric that clogs the fibers, trapping moisture and bacteria inside the absorbent core. Always allow them to air-dry completely before putting them away!

How do I wash and care for Hailaflo period underwear?

Caring for your reusable period underwear is simple! Proper care ensures that your 4-layer leak-proof technology stays highly effective and lasts for years. Just follow these four easy steps:

  • Step 1: Rinse. After wearing, rinse your underwear under cold running water or soak them until the water runs clear.
  • Step 2: Wash. Pop them in the washing machine on a cold, gentle cycle (30°C to 40°C). You can wash them with your normal laundry.
  • Step 3: Skip the Additives. Do not use fabric softener, bleach, or stain removers. These can coat the fibers and destroy the built-in absorbent core.
  • Step 4: Air Dry. Hang your underwear to air dry completely. Avoid tumble dryers and direct radiator heat, as high heat can damage the waterproof leak-proof barrier.
How long will my HailaFlo garments last?

With proper care, Hailaflo period underwear can last up to 4 years! On average, our advanced 4-layer technology is built to maintain its maximum, high-performance absorbency for at least 50 to 100 washes.

How long they last in your wardrobe depends on your laundry rotation:

  • Large Rotation (Lasts 4+ Years): If you own a full set (5–7 pairs) and only wash each pair once per cycle, they will easily last up to 4 years or longer.
  • Small Rotation (Lasts 3 Years): If you own a smaller collection (3–4 pairs) and wash and wear them multiple times during a single period week, they will still reliably last you up to 3 years.

Even after hitting their maximum wash milestone, your Hailaflo underwear won't suddenly stop working. The built-in fabric tech will simply begin to very slowly decline in absorbency. When you eventually notice this change, you can seamlessly retire them to be used on your lighter flow days, for spotting, or as a backup to a menstrual cup.

Do period pants feel wet when you’re wearing them?

No, Hailaflo period underwear does not feel wet while you are wearing it.

The secret lies in our advanced 4-layer leak-proof technology. The very first layer—the moisture-wicking top layer—is made from a high-performance fabric that pulls liquid away from your skin instantly.

Once the fluid passes through this top layer, it is locked tightly inside the absorbent core beneath it. Because the surface dries almost immediately, you won’t experience that cold, damp, or sticky sensation often associated with traditional disposable sanitary pads.

Why might they ever feel wet?

  • They have reached full capacity: If you start to feel moisture on the top layer, it is simply a sign that the core is completely full and it is time to change into a fresh pair.
  • The fit is too loose: If your underwear doesn't fit snugly against your body, the moisture-wicking layer cannot pull the fluid away efficiently.

Cycle & Everyday Use

How Many Pairs of Period Underwear Do I Need?

If you are making the switch to sustainable period care, a great starting point for a full menstrual cycle is 5 to 7 pairs of reusable period underwear.

Because your flow changes throughout the week, your perfect number depends entirely on your unique cycle, your laundry habits, and how you plan to use them.

Choose Your Routine

1. The Full Switch (5 to 7 Pairs)

  • Best For: Committing to an entirely eco-friendly period without doing laundry every single day.
  • The Routine: You wear 2 pairs per day (one for daytime and one for overnight protection), rinsing them after use and washing the entire set at the end of your cycle.

2. The Part-Time Switch (3 to 4 Pairs)

3. The Backup / Starter Option (1 to 2 Pairs)

  • Best For: Beginners wanting to test our advanced 4-layer technology, or anyone looking for extra leak-proof protection alongside a menstrual cup or tampon.

Does wearing period underwear change your period or make it lighter?

No, wearing period underwear cannot change your menstrual cycle or make your flow lighter.

Your period is regulated entirely by your internal hormones, your genetics, and your reproductive health. No external menstrual product—whether it is a disposable pad, a tampon, a menstrual cup, or reusable period underwear—has the ability to influence your actual internal uterine lining shedding.

However, many women who switch to Hailaflo report that their periods feel lighter, shorter, and much more comfortable. Here is why:

  • Instant Absorption: Hailaflo’s advanced 4-layer technology uses an instant-dry moisture-wicking top layer. Because it pulls fluid away from your skin immediately, you don't feel the blood pooling, creating the sensation of a lighter, cleaner cycle.
  • 0% Plastic-Backed Stickiness: Conventional disposable pads use plastic backings that trap heat, moisture, and sweat. This can cause fluid to smear and pool, making your flow look and feel much heavier than it actually is.
  • Toxin-Free Comfort: Conventional products often contain synthetic fragrances and chemical bleaching agents that can irritate your skin and increase cramping discomfort. Switching to OEKO-TEX® certified underwear removes that chemical irritation completely.
