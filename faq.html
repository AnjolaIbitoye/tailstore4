Hailaflo Period Underwear: Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about absorbency, fit, materials, care, and everyday use.

Product Basics & Protection

What is Hailaflo Period Underwear and how does it work?

Hailaflo Period Underwear is reusable period underwear designed to replace traditional pads and tampons. Its built-in, 4-layer gusset absorbs your flow directly, giving you peace of mind and sustainable comfort throughout the day.

Four high-performance layers work together to keep you dry, fresh, and confident:

  • Moisture-Wicking Top Layer: Rapidly pulls fluid away from your skin so you feel dry, fresh, and comfortable.
  • Absorbent Core: Traps and locks in liquid using capillary action to handle everything from light spotting to heavy flow.
  • Waterproof Leak-Proof Barrier: Prevents blood and fluid from seeping through and staining clothes and bedsheets.
  • Premium Outer Fabric Layer: Provides the final structural layer of security while looking and feeling like high-quality, everyday underwear.

Switch to Hailaflo for reliable, sustainable period care designed to protect you and the planet.

Can period underwear leak?

Hailaflo period underwear is designed to provide leak-proof protection. Like any menstrual product, it can leak if it reaches full capacity or fits poorly. Choose the right fit and absorbency for your cycle.

How do I find the perfect fit?

For leak protection, period underwear should fit snugly around your legs and waist without digging in. Loose leg openings can leave gaps. Check the size guide and measure your hips to find your best fit.

Materials & Safety

What does OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 certification mean?

OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 is one of the best-known labels for textiles tested for harmful substances. Hailaflo states that every component of its reusable period underwear—from the moisture-wicking top layer to threads and elastics—is certified under this standard and tested for harmful substances. Check current product and certification information for details about a specific style.

Does Hailaflo contain PFAS?

Hailaflo states that its period underwear is free from detectable PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances). Its products hold OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 certification, which tests textiles against a list of harmful substances.

Rather than relying on synthetic chemical treatments for waterproofing or stain resistance, Hailaflo's leak-protection barriers and absorbent cores use the physical weave and capillary action of the fabrics.

Does Hailaflo use nanotechnology?

Core performance is primarily delivered through layered textile engineering. Any additional fabric treatment is disclosed in product specifications.

Care & Performance

Will my period underwear smell while I wear it?

Hailaflo period underwear is designed with breathable, moisture-wicking fabrics to help keep you feeling fresh. The supplied product guidance says it can be worn for up to 8 to 12 hours, depending on flow and absorbency. Change into a fresh pair when needed.

  • Moisture-Wicking Top Layer: Pulls menstrual fluid away from your skin and into the absorbent core.
  • Managing moisture helps reduce the damp environment where odour can develop.

Pro-tip to keep them fresh: Do not use fabric softeners. They can leave residue that clogs fabric fibres. Allow underwear to air-dry completely before storing.

How do I wash and care for Hailaflo period underwear?

Proper care helps maintain the absorbent layers:

  • Rinse: Rinse under cold running water or soak until the water runs clear.
  • Wash: Machine wash on a cold, gentle cycle. The supplied care guidance allows 30°C to 40°C.
  • Skip additives: Do not use fabric softener, bleach, or stain removers.
  • Air dry: Hang to dry completely. Avoid tumble dryers and direct radiator heat.
How long will my Hailaflo garments last?

With proper care, Hailaflo period underwear can last up to 4 years. The supplied guidance estimates 50 to 100 washes before maximum absorbency may begin to decline. Actual lifespan depends on your laundry rotation and care routine.

  • Large rotation (5–7 pairs): Washing each pair less often may help them last 4 years or longer.
  • Small rotation (3–4 pairs): More frequent washing may mean a useful lifespan of around 3 years.

The underwear will not suddenly stop working after that point; absorbency may gradually decline. Pairs can then be used for lighter-flow days, spotting, or as backup.

Do period pants feel wet when you are wearing them?

No, Hailaflo period underwear is designed not to feel wet while you are wearing it. The moisture-wicking top layer pulls liquid away from your skin and into the absorbent core. The surface is designed to dry quickly, helping avoid a cold, damp, or sticky feeling.

Why might they ever feel wet?

  • They have reached full capacity: If the top layer begins to feel damp, change into a fresh pair.
  • The fit is too loose: A snug fit helps the moisture-wicking layer move fluid away efficiently.

Cycle & Everyday Use

How many pairs of period underwear do I need?

For a full menstrual cycle, a good starting point is 5 to 7 pairs. The number you need depends on your flow, laundry routine, and how you plan to use them.

  • Full switch (5 to 7 pairs): Wear period underwear throughout your cycle without doing laundry every day. The supplied routine suggests changing pairs during the day and washing the set at the end of your cycle.
  • Part-time switch (3 to 4 pairs): Wear them on heavier days, overnight, or with quick mid-week laundry.
  • Backup or starter option (1 to 2 pairs): Try the 4-layer design or use a pair as backup with a menstrual cup or tampon.

Does wearing period underwear change your period or make it lighter?

No. Period underwear cannot change your menstrual cycle or make your flow lighter. Your cycle is regulated by your hormones, genetics, and reproductive health; an external menstrual product cannot change the shedding of your uterine lining.

Some wearers say their period feels lighter, shorter, or more comfortable in period underwear. That can be a difference in how the product feels, not a change in the amount or length of menstrual bleeding.

  • Instant absorption: The moisture-wicking top layer is designed to move fluid away from your skin, which may reduce the feeling of pooling.
  • Everyday comfort: Some people find fabric underwear less bulky or sticky than some disposable products. Comfort varies by person and product.
  • Materials: Check the product's certification and materials information for details. Textile certification does not guarantee that a product will prevent irritation for everyone.
What should I expect on my first day wearing Hailaflo?

Trying reusable period underwear for the first time can feel unfamiliar. Here is a simple guide to getting started:

🌅 Morning: Putting Them On

  • What it feels like: Like a pair of everyday underwear.
  • The fit: Aim for a secure fit around the waist and leg openings, without digging in. A close fit helps the gusset sit against your body.
  • The silhouette: Period underwear is designed to be worn like underwear, without a disposable pad attached.

☀️ Afternoon: The Dryness Check

  • What it feels like: The moisture-wicking layer is designed to move fluid into the absorbent core.
  • Check in: Pay attention to your comfort and flow. If the surface feels damp or the gusset feels full, change into a fresh pair.

🌆 Evening: Changing and Rinsing

  • When to change: Wear time varies with flow, fit, and absorbency. Hailaflo's guidance suggests up to 8–12 hours, but change sooner if needed.
  • The cleanup: Rinse the gusset under cold tap water and gently squeeze until the water runs clear, then add the pair to your laundry.
