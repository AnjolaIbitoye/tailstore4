Shipping, Returns & Exchanges

What to expect when your Hailaflo order is on its way, and how returns and exchanges work.

Shipping & Delivery

We want to get your sustainable period care to you as quickly as possible!

  • Processing & Shipping: All orders are processed, packed, and shipped fast within 1–2 business days.
  • Discreet Packaging: To respect your privacy, all Hailaflo orders are delivered in plain, unbranded packaging.
  • Shipping Rates: Free standard shipping is available on purchase of 3 items. Standard shipping rates apply for smaller orders.

Returns & Exchanges Policy

To comply with strict health and safety regulations, our reusable period underwear is classified as a hygiene-sensitive item.

  • Opened or Tried-On Items: Due to strict health protection and hygiene reasons, any period underwear that has been opened, tried on, or has a broken hygiene seal cannot be returned or exchanged.
  • Unopened & Sealed Items: You have 30 days from the date of delivery to request a return or exchange for a full refund. To be eligible, the item must be completely unworn, unwashed, and remain 100% sealed in its original product packaging.
  • Damaged or Defective Items: If your item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us immediately. We will arrange a replacement or refund right away.
